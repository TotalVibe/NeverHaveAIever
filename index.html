How To Play 🔥

😈 Spicy (UP)

Dating secrets, romantic fails, guilty pleasures & intimate truths.

😳 Cringe (LEFT)

Hilarious fails, caught red-handed moments & public embarrassment.

🥳 Party (RIGHT)

Nightlife chaos, drinking dares, wild nights out & club adventures.

😇 Wholesome (DOWN)

Childhood memories, weird daily habits, food quirks & relatable life.

🎮 Controls:

  • Swipe / Drag Card: Drag the active card towards or on top of any of the 4 emojis to choose that topic for the next card.
  • Tap Emoji: Directly tap any of the 4 emoji buttons to immediately choose that topic!
  • Keyboard: Arrow Keys (↑ Spicy, ← Cringe, → Party, ↓ Wholesome), Spacebar to shuffle, Z to undo.
