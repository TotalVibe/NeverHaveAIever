Privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Never Have AI Ever is a card game. This page explains what the game stores, what it sends, and what it doesn't.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics.

Your group description

If you type a description of your group in Customize, the text is only used to show a placeholder. It is never saved or sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

The game remembers your chosen card intensity (Cheeky, Spicy or Unhinged) in your browser's local storage. This value never leaves your device. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Advertising

The game currently shows no ads. In the future we plan to show a short video ad after you save your preferences in Customize. If that happens, the ad provider may use cookies or device identifiers to show and measure the ad. This page will be updated before any ads go live.

Third-party services

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When a font loads, Google receives standard connection details such as your IP address, as it does for any website that uses Google Fonts. The game sends nothing else to third parties.
